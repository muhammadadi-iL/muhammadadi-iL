## 🚀 **About Me**
Hey there! I'm a **Senior Backend Developer** with more than **five years** of experience in **PHP, Laravel, and MySQL** — over four of them professionally building production web applications, **REST APIs**, and multi-role platforms.

I've shipped backends for **marketplaces, booking systems, e-commerce, healthcare, and SaaS**: payment flows, wallets, order lifecycles, and the architecture that keeps them maintainable. On most recent projects I was the only backend developer from empty repository to delivery; before that I led a backend team of up to 4 developers across 8 production platforms.

Beyond the backend, I work comfortably with **React, Vue, Livewire, and Next.js**, and I integrate real-time and cloud pieces — **Pusher, Firebase, Redis, queues, and Docker** — so the system stays fast under load.

💡 **Passionate About:**  
✔ Building **highly scalable web applications**  
✔ Architecting **RESTful APIs & multi-role platforms**  
✔ Crafting **secure authentication, RBAC & payment systems**  
✔ Implementing **real-time experiences with WebSockets & Pusher**  
✔ Deploying & optimizing apps on **AWS, Vercel, VPS, and Docker**
